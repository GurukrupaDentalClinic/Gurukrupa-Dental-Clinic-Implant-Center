GURUKRUPA DENTAL CLINIC SINCE 2007 — one stop solution for all type of dental treatment. Your smile is our goal. We work here for your happiness & smile.


With over 19 years of experience, Dr. Satish Modi has been providing trusted, ethical, and high-quality dental care since 2007. His approach focuses on accurate diagnosis, personalized treatment, and patient comfort—so every patient receives the best possible care.

Dr. Modi believes that a healthy smile is the foundation of confidence and overall well-being. He stays updated with modern dental techniques and technologies to deliver safe, effective, and painless treatments.

About Our Practice

Established in 2007, our dental practice has been committed to delivering comprehensive dental care with honesty, compassion, and excellence. We offer a wide range of preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and implant dentistry services in a clean, comfortable, and patient-friendly environment.

Our mission is to build lasting relationships with our patients by providing quality treatment, transparent guidance, and personalized care. Thousands of patients have trusted us with their smiles, and we continue to serve our community with dedication and professionalism.
